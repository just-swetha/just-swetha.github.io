About

I'm a communication strategist and multimedia designer working at the intersection of story, research, and emerging media. Currently pursuing an MA in Emerging Media Design and Development at Ball State University, I develop outreach and communication strategy for Sponsored Projects Administration while building interactive narratives, visual novels, and transmedia experiences. My background in fiction writing and narrative design shapes how I approach every medium. I bring skills across platforms and formats to meet audiences where they are.
